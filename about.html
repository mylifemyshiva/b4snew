About B4S Group

Pursuit of excellence in security and housekeeping manpower.

Experienced staff, disciplined service

B4S Group Security Services is run by an experienced team and is ready to take on government, semi-government and private complexes, high-tech housing, hospitals and industrial sites.

Our aim is to create a secure condition for management, staff and workers so they can carry on their tasks without fear.

We offer a security culture that binds your set-up and our organisation in a lasting relationship, security consultancy, research and survey, and trained, motivated manpower at competitive rates.

How we work

Enrolment

We select people on physical fitness, educational level, mental make-up, character verification and bearing.

Training

Experienced staff train every guard before deployment, with continuing training and housekeeping management. Gunmen practise regularly at firing ranges.

Response

Clients and officials can reach us day and night. Our officers carry mobile phones for immediate action.

Site survey

Once a contract is awarded, we survey your building and discuss a draft report with you.
